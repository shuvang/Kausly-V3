FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does “outsourced IT department” actually mean?

We take full ownership of your technology — procurement, maintenance, management, monitoring and strategy — exactly as an in-house IT team would, for a fraction of the cost. Your team gets one point of contact for everything IT.

What's included in the $1,000/month starting price?

All five services — procurement, maintenance, management, consulting and 24/7 monitoring — scoped to a small team. Your exact fee depends on the number of users and systems we cover, and it stays flat month to month.

We already have some IT in place. How do you take over?

Onboarding starts with a full audit of your environment. We document everything, stabilise what's fragile, and transition management without disrupting your team — typically within two to four weeks.

How fast do you respond when something breaks?

Our average response time is 15 minutes, and most issues are caught by our monitoring before anyone notices. Critical incidents are worked continuously until resolved.

Are we locked into a long contract?

No. We work on a monthly rolling agreement. We'd rather earn your business every month than lock you into a term — and our clients stay because it works.
