FAQ

Questions, answered.

What's included in the $1,000/month starting price?

All four services — procurement, maintenance, management and consulting — plus 24/7 monitoring, scoped to a small team. Your exact fee depends on the users and systems we cover, and it stays flat month to month.

How is my exact price calculated?

Primarily by the number of users and the complexity of your systems (servers, locations, compliance needs). We quote after a short audit — and the quote is the price, with no hourly extras.

Are we locked into a long contract?

No. We work on a monthly rolling agreement. We'd rather earn your business every month than lock you into a term.

What if we grow — or shrink?

Your plan scales with your headcount and systems. We review the scope quarterly, and you can request a re-scope any month.

Is hardware included in the fee?

The service of procuring it is; the hardware itself is billed at the negotiated vendor price with no markup. You see every quote we receive.
